Base Hover Card composes Root, Trigger, and Content. Root supports controlled or uncontrolled open state, `openDelay`, `closeDelay`, and `openChange`; Content uses `asOverlay` plus `asTransition` and detaches only after leave completes. Positioning currently guarantees `side` and `align`; portal, collision handling, Arrow, forceMount, and numeric offsets remain deferred.
